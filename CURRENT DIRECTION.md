## Practical 90-minute learning session

1. 10 minutes: select one output and a fictional or owned input. Define buyer, required inputs and acceptance criteria.
2. 15 minutes: inspect an example and learn what makes it correct and useful.
3. 25 minutes: build one sample together; explain the steps in plain language.
4. 20 minutes: user reviews facts, tone, formatting and completeness, then makes a requested revision.
5. 20 minutes: repeat with fresh input and record time, difficulty and unanswered questions.

Passing means the user understands the output, can catch errors and can repeat delivery. It does not establish market demand. If the exercise is uncomfortable or too difficult, change the scope or candidate. Only then draft a small offer with inputs, exact output, turnaround, revision limit and exclusions; price remains a proposal until approved.
