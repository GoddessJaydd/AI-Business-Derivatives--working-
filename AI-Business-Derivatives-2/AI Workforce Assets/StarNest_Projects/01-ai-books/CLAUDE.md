## External APIs & Integrations

Before building scrapers, collecting structured data manually at scale, purchasing data tools, or creating custom integrations:

1. Read [../SHARED/API_CATALOG.md](../SHARED/API_CATALOG.md).
2. Follow [../SHARED/API_SELECTION_RULES.md](../SHARED/API_SELECTION_RULES.md).
3. Review [../SHARED/INTEGRATION_IDEAS.md](../SHARED/INTEGRATION_IDEAS.md) for project-relevant experiments.
4. Prefer official APIs and suitable official MCP servers; favor free or low-cost validation.
5. Verify current availability, pricing, quotas, authentication, licensing, and commercial-use terms before implementation. Catalog entries are not automatically approved.
6. Keep API keys in environment variables or a secret manager, never Markdown or source control.
7. Record evidence and decisions in the shared catalog rather than duplicating provider lists here.
