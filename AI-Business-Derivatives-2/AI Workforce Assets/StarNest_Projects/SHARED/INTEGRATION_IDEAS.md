# StarNest Integration Ideas

These are hypotheses to evaluate, not active integrations or claims about provider capabilities. Use [API_CATALOG.md](API_CATALOG.md) and [API_SELECTION_RULES.md](API_SELECTION_RULES.md) before choosing a service.

| Projects | Small first experiment | Evidence of value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI books, ghostwriting | Enrich one research entry with source metadata and links | Less research time with traceable sources |
| Creators, Shorts, evergreen and faceless YouTube | Summarize analytics from one authorized channel | Accurate report with source links and less manual copying |
| Clipping | Test a permitted caption workflow on one owned clip | Caption accuracy and processing cost |
| Landing pages | Connect one test form to an approved destination | Correct delivery and understandable failure handling |
| Etsy POD, digital templates | Explore one product or fulfillment workflow in a test environment | Correct product data and clear per-order costs |
| AI agents, AI apps | Connect one read-only tool to a specific validated need | Useful result with minimal permissions |
| Opportunity Scout, affiliate search | Retrieve a small dated set of opportunities or approved product data | Freshness, coverage, and traceable sources |
| Social media management, GBP optimization | Produce a report for one authorized account | Verified metrics and time saved |
| AI training | Turn a small permitted survey export into a training needs summary | Accurate findings and appropriate data handling |

## Prioritization
Prefer a clear user need, measurable time saved, low test cost, usable data rights, and a workable fallback. Record the chosen experiment's owner, source, date, expected cost, success criterion, result, and next action. Expand only after checking the evidence.
