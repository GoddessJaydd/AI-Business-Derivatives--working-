# Maintenance Communication Workflow Audit — 60 minutes

Updated October 4, 2026. Follow-on diagnostic, offered only when a workshop reveals a specific need. **Proposed price: $300 USD, not approved**, within the earlier $250–$500 draft range.

## Buyer, duration, and narrow outcome

**Buyer:** A property-management owner or office manager who wants to improve the maintenance-note-to-owner-update workflow.  
**Duration:** **60-minute remote diagnostic** with one responsible owner and, optionally, one staff representative.  
**Outcome:** Map one communication workflow, identify its biggest avoidable delay or rework point, and choose a manual improvement or one small AI-assisted pilot. This is not a full-company audit.

## Deliverables

- One current-state workflow map with inputs, handoffs, decisions, and output.
- One evidence table: current step, pain point, possible improvement, estimated impact, complexity, data constraints, and priority.
- A one-to-two-page recommendation within one business day.
- One pilot specification or a reasoned no-go: owner, permitted inputs, expected output, acceptance checks, and stop condition.
- One clarification response about the report within seven days.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Work |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Choose the recurring communication problem and desired outcome |
| 10–25 | Walk through up to three anonymized cases and current handoffs |
| 25–40 | Identify missing facts, approvals, rework, sensitive data, and decision ownership |
| 40–50 | Compare a better manual template with an AI-assisted draft workflow |
| 50–60 | Select a bounded test or no-go, accountable owner, and next action |

## Questions and evidence

What notes arrive, who checks them, who authorizes cost or schedule changes, and who sends the update? Where do requests wait? Which facts are frequently missing? What must never enter an external model? How often does this occur, and is time spent measured or estimated? Would a shared template solve it without AI?

Keep estimates labeled. Do not invent hours saved, customer satisfaction, or revenue. Use anonymized examples only; no system access or confidential tenant case review is required.

**Complexity / data constraints:** [reasoned assessment]

**Pilot:** five anonymized cases; compare factual accuracy and draft-plus-review effort  
**Acceptance:** no unsupported approval, cost, schedule, or completion claim after review; owner can repeat the process  
**Stop:** unresolved factual errors, prohibited data use, or review effort that removes the benefit  
**Next action / owner / date:** [one bounded step]

## Exclusions and effort limit

No software implementation, property-system access, inbox access/sending, full operational audit, legal/tax/compliance advice, data-protection certification, guaranteed ROI, or ongoing consulting. Target 60 minutes live plus up to 45 minutes preparation/reporting; log actual effort. Any implementation needs its own validated scope and approval.
