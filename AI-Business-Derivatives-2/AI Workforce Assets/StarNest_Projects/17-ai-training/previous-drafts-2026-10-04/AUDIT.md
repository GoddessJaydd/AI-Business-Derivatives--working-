# Workflow Audit Template (follow-on to the workshop)

Status: DRAFT. Offer only after a workshop shows a clear need.

## Goal
Find where AI could save meaningful time, then rank the opportunities.

## Interview questions (30 to 45 min)
1. Walk me through a typical week. Which tasks repeat?
2. Where do handoffs stall or get duplicated?
3. Which writing or research tasks take the longest?
4. What mistakes or rework happen most?
5. What data is confidential and must never go into AI tools?

## Deliverable: one table
Current state -> Pain point -> Possible AI use case -> Estimated impact (hours/week, as an estimate) -> Complexity (low/med/high) -> Priority

## Rules
- Estimates are labeled estimates, not guarantees.
- No promises of specific savings or revenue.
- Recommend implementation only for validated, low-risk use cases; otherwise stop at the roadmap.

## Delivery checklist
- [ ] Signed scope and what's excluded
- [ ] Interview notes
- [ ] Draft table reviewed with the client
- [ ] Final one-to-two page roadmap
- [ ] Hours spent logged
