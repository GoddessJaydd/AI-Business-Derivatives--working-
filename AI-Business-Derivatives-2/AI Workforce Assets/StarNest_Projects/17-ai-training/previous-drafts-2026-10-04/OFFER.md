# AI Training Offer: "AI Workday Tune-Up"

Status: DRAFT for founder approval. Prices below are PROPOSALS, not approved or tested.

## One-sentence outcome
In 90 minutes, your team learns to use Claude or ChatGPT safely on the tasks they already do, and leaves with 3 to 5 reusable templates and a short list of where AI could save the most time next.

## Buyer (starting niche)
Small office-based businesses of roughly 3 to 25 people with repetitive written and admin work: property managers, small accounting/tax firms, and small real estate offices. Starting geography: Oakland/East Bay and Mariposa/Yosemite area.
Why: they handle high volumes of emails, owner/client statements, listings, and follow-ups, and they are small enough that the owner can say yes without committee approval. This is a hypothesis to test, not proven demand.

## What they get
1. Pre-call (15 min): which tasks eat the most time.
2. Live 60 to 90 minute workshop (remote or in person) using their own non-confidential example tasks.
3. A one-page "how we use AI here" guide with do and don't rules (confidential data, client names, accuracy checks).
4. 3 to 5 ready-to-use prompt templates built around their real tasks.
5. A 7-day email follow-up for questions.

## Not included
Building automations, connecting software, legal/tax/compliance advice, handling client confidential data, or ongoing management. Those are later steps (workflow audit, then implementation) and only after the workshop shows a real need.

## Proposed pricing (unapproved, to test)
- Pilot workshop (first 2 clients, in exchange for a testimonial only if they genuinely agree): $150 to $300
- Standard team workshop (up to 8 people): $400 to $600
- Add-on workflow audit (see AUDIT.md): $250 to $500
Decision needed from founder: choose exact numbers before the first paid ask (Day 4).

## Service ladder
Workshop -> Workflow audit -> Consulting roadmap -> Implementation (only after validated need).

## Success test (from master context)
One paid engagement that produces a measurable improvement and a repeatable delivery process.
