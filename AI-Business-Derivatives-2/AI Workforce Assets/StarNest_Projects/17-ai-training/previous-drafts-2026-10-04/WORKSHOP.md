# Workshop Outline: AI Workday Tune-Up (90 minutes)

Status: DRAFT. Customize roughly 20% per client; keep the rest standard.

## Before the session (pre-call, 15 min)
- Ask: what 3 tasks take the most time each week? What tools do you already use? Anyone already using AI?
- Collect 2 to 3 non-confidential sample tasks (blank templates, redacted examples).

## Agenda
| Time | Segment | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:10 | What AI is good and bad at | Set realistic expectations; hallucinations and checking |
| 0:10-0:25 | Giving context (the 4-part prompt: role, task, context, format) | Core skill |
| 0:25-0:55 | Hands-on with THEIR tasks (emails, summaries, listings or statements, follow-ups) | Immediate value |
| 0:55-1:10 | Safe-use rules: confidential data, client names, review before sending | Responsible use |
| 1:10-1:20 | Build 3 to 5 reusable templates together | Takeaway asset |
| 1:20-1:30 | Q&A and next step (audit offer) | Ladder |

## Deliverables after the session
- One-page "how we use AI here" guide
- 3 to 5 prompt templates
- 7-day follow-up email

## Delivery checklist
- [ ] Confirm date, attendees, remote/in-person
- [ ] Get sample tasks 48 hours ahead
- [ ] Test screen share and a demo prompt
- [ ] Deliver guide + templates within 24 hours
- [ ] Log delivery hours and cash collected in the sales log
- [ ] Ask (do not assume) for a testimonial or referral
- [ ] Note what was slow so it can become the repeatable SOP

## Demonstration to prepare (proof artifact)
Pick one sample task (for example: turning a rough owner-update note into a clear email, or summarizing a long email thread) and record a short before/after you can show prospects. Use only invented sample text.
