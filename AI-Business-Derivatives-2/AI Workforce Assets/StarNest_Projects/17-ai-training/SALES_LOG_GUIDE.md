# Dated sales log — ready to use

Created October 4, 2026 in the existing StarNest 30-Day Money Plan.

**Open:** [Sales Log 2026-10-04](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eX3n2sKstb8W3y9hH71iP8QFIflOcsosW91nkcVYs70/edit#gid=492322426)

One row per prospect/deal. Leave the log empty until a real prospect is researched. Creating the tab does not mean outreach happened or a sale was made. Never add the fictional cleaning business from the demo as a real lead.

| Column | Entry rule |
| --- | --- |
| Logged date | Date the real prospect/deal was first added |
| Prospect | Business and relevant person's name, when known |
| Source | Source URL or legitimate referral source; do not invent contact data |
| Channel | Warm referral, Email, LinkedIn, Phone, In person, or Other |
| First contact | Date an authorized message or conversation actually occurred; blank before outreach |
| Follow-up | Next agreed/planned follow-up date; update after action |
| Reply | Dated factual note, including no reply only when that status is known |
| Sample | Link or filename, date, and whether prepared or shared |
| Call | Scheduled/completed status and date; do not count a proposed call as booked |
| Proposal | Scope/version, approval status, date, link; add payment reference after payment |
| Amount (USD) | Latest proposed/agreed total deal value; not earned revenue |
| Cash collected (USD) | Successful customer payments less refunds, before fees; exclude tests and unpaid invoices |
| Delivery hours | Actual preparation, live session, and follow-up hours; decimals such as 1.75 |
| Next action | One action, owner, and due date; use Closed if no further action is planned |

Enter dates as YYYY-MM-DD, interpreted in America/Los_Angeles. The workbook itself retains its existing Etc/GMT setting; do not change the timezone of the other plan tabs. Blank means not recorded, not zero. For a known unpaid prospect, cash may be entered as zero once appropriate. Keep evidence and dates in the notes fields so later updates remain understandable.

The log has a header row, typed date/currency/hour columns, a Channel field, and 200 prepared blank entry rows. The attempted channel-dropdown update was not executed because automatic approval review could not complete after a usage-limit error. All six existing planning tabs are preserved. Do not log credentials, card information, or confidential customer inquiries.
