# Inquiry Reply Workflow Audit — 60-minute diagnostic

Prepared October 4, 2026. Optional diagnostic scope, not the primary sales offer. **Proposed price: $95 USD; not approved.** Do not bundle or charge for it automatically.

## Buyer, narrow outcome, and duration

**Buyer:** An owner-operated home-service business receiving repeated written inquiries whose owner wants to decide whether an AI-assisted reply workflow is worth testing.

**Outcome:** Map one inquiry-to-reply process and deliver a one-page recommendation: run a small draft-only pilot, fix the manual process first, or do not use AI for this workflow yet.

**Duration:** **60-minute remote diagnostic** with the owner. Allow up to 30 minutes internal preparation/report completion; target total delivery effort of 90 minutes. Deliver the written recommendation within one business day.

## Deliverables

- One current-state map: inquiry arrives → facts checked → missing details requested → quote/booking handoff → human sends reply.
- A short evidence table identifying repeated work, missing business rules, sensitive data, and error consequences.
- One prioritized improvement and one draft-only pilot specification, or a reasoned no-go recommendation.
- One-page report with owner, input, proposed output, success criterion, estimated effort assumptions, and next action.
- One clarification response about the report within seven days.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Work | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Define the specific business problem and desired result | Owner-stated goal; current volume if known |
| 10–25 | Walk through up to three anonymized inquiries | Actual steps, business rules, decisions |
| 25–40 | Identify repeatable drafting work and where judgment is required | Bottleneck and risk notes |
| 40–50 | Compare manual-template improvement with an AI-assisted draft | Reasoned recommendation and assumptions |
| 50–60 | Agree a small test or no-go, owner, and next action | One bounded experiment, not a transformation roadmap |

## Intake questions

1. Roughly how many written inquiries arrive each week, and through which channel?
2. What does a typical reply require you to look up?
3. What information is regularly missing?
4. Which claims require your personal approval: price, availability, service area, special work?
5. How long do replies take? Is that measured or estimated?
6. What customer data appears, and what may be used in the selected AI tool?
7. Would a saved manual reply template already solve most of the problem?

Unknown values remain unknown. Record owner estimates as estimates. Request examples only after removing names, contact details, addresses, and confidential content. No live inbox access is needed.
