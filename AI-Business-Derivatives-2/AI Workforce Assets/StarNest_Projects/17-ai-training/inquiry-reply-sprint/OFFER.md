# StarNest AI Inquiry Reply Sprint — operating offer

Prepared October 4, 2026. **Draft commercial offer; $125 USD pilot price is proposed and not approved.** Nothing in this file authorizes publishing an offer, contacting prospects, or collecting payment.

## Buyer and narrow outcome

**Buyer:** An owner-operated cleaning, landscaping, or similar home-service business that receives written quote inquiries and personally handles replies. Start with one owner and one business; prioritize warm contacts with recurring inquiry volume.

**Outcome:** In a **75-minute, one-to-one remote session**, the owner builds and practices a repeatable process for turning a customer inquiry into a clear, factual reply draft using their existing AI account. The process asks for missing information and leaves quotes, scheduling, and sending under the owner's control. It does not promise more leads, faster sales, or a specific time saving.

**Definition of done:** The buyer can use the supplied prompt on one new inquiry, distinguish known facts from missing details, and approve or correct the resulting draft without the facilitator writing it for them. The take-home materials are complete even if the buyer needs further practice.

## Scope and deliverables

- One 75-minute live working session for one business owner.
- One business fact card: actual services, confirmed service area, tone, and quoting/booking rules.
- One reusable inquiry-reply prompt and a human-review checklist.
- Three worked practice cases: ordinary inquiry, missing details, and a request outside confirmed scope.
- One page of instructions the owner can reuse without new software.
- A short action recap sent within one business day of the session.
- One clarification response about the supplied materials within seven calendar days; no ongoing support retainer.

Use the agenda in `WORKSHOP.md` and the synthetic example in `PROOF_SAMPLE.md`. Those are delivery materials for this same offer, not extra products the buyer must purchase.

## Inputs and fit check

Before accepting payment, confirm the buyer, scope, proposed session time and timezone, price, and cancellation terms in writing. Ask the buyer for a short approved business fact list and two or three anonymized sample inquiries. Do not request inbox access, passwords, payment details, customer addresses, or client account credentials. The buyer should have access to an AI tool they are allowed to use for this task; no paid upgrade is required for the proposed scope.

Not a fit if the buyer needs autonomous inbox management, regulated advice, technical integrations, or a guarantee that the model will never make mistakes. If the buyer has little inquiry volume, do not oversell a reply workflow as a major operational transformation.

## Duration and delivery budget

Buyer session: 75 minutes. Internal allowance: up to 20 minutes preparation and 25 minutes recap/clarification administration, approximately two hours total. Record actual delivery hours in the sales log. If preparation expands beyond this, narrow the example set or revise the offer before promising additional work.

## Exclusions

No live email sending, CRM connection, automation build, chatbot, website, brand suite, lead generation, outsourced customer service, actual quoting/booking, legal/financial advice, unlimited revisions, or guaranteed commercial result. No recording by default; obtain explicit consent if a recording is later requested. Do not upload confidential customer information into an unapproved AI service.
