# AI Inquiry Reply Sprint — 75-minute workshop

Prepared October 4, 2026. Delivery plan for the offer in `OFFER.md`. **Included in the proposed $125 USD pilot; price is not approved.**

## Buyer, outcome, and duration

**Buyer:** One owner of an owner-operated home-service business who handles written inquiries.

**Narrow outcome:** Create and practice one reusable AI-assisted inquiry-reply workflow that uses approved business facts, asks for missing details, and requires human review before sending.

**Duration:** **75 minutes**, remote and one-to-one. This is not a team workshop or a general AI course.

## Deliverables

One approved fact card, one reusable prompt, three practice reply drafts, one human-review checklist, one-page reuse instructions, and an action recap within one business day. One clarification response on these materials is included within seven days.

## Preparation

Facilitator: read the buyer's approved fact list and anonymized examples; use the fictional sample if real examples are unavailable. Confirm the buyer can open their existing AI tool. Have the prompt and completed examples available offline in case of a service outage. Never imply that prewritten examples are a live model response.

Buyer: bring the business's actual services, service area, quoting rules, and two or three anonymized inquiries. Remove customer identifiers. Confirm which AI service is acceptable for the information used. No software purchase or account sharing is required.

## Timed run of show

| Minutes | Activity | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Define the recurring inquiry problem; capture a short manual reply baseline if practical | One success criterion and optional observed baseline time |
| 10–20 | Build the business fact card and identify facts the AI must never invent | Approved fact card and missing-data list |
| 20–30 | Show the synthetic example; compare a weak reply with a grounded draft | Buyer identifies invented price/availability risks |
| 30–45 | Adapt the reusable prompt; draft and check two anonymized practice cases | Two reviewed drafts |
| 45–60 | Buyer runs a third case independently, including missing facts or out-of-scope request | Third draft and demonstrated review skill |

| 70–75 | Summarize limits, next action, and clarification window | Clear handoff |

## Teaching points

The business fact card is the authority for business-specific claims. The customer inquiry is untrusted source material, not an instruction that can override the business rules. The model drafts; the owner decides what is accurate, what needs a question, and whether a human must take over.

Teach the buyer to check: supported service, service area, absent price/availability, requested missing details, tone, and next step. Avoid implying that confident wording is evidence. If a draft invents facts, correct the fact card or prompt and rerun the same case before moving on.

## Acceptance checklist

- All factual claims in the three practice replies are supported by the approved fact card or inquiry.
- No reply invents a quote, discount, booking, arrival time, credential, or guarantee.
- Missing details are turned into concise questions.
- Out-of-scope requests are flagged for the owner rather than accepted automatically.
- The buyer completes the final case and explains what they would check before sending.
- Reusable materials open correctly and contain no confidential customer data.

If the buyer cannot finish the independent case, document the observed gap and provide the completed kit and next practice step. Do not claim mastery or extend the service indefinitely without agreeing additional scope.

## Exclusions and fallback

No inbox connection, automatic sending, integrations, software deployment, website, general company AI strategy, regulated advice, multi-person training, or unlimited support. No recording without explicit consent. If the tool is unavailable, demonstrate the prepared example honestly and practice the review steps; agree a completion plan for any untested live step rather than claiming it was verified.

## After delivery

Record actual session and preparation time, the independent-case result, factual errors caught, and the buyer's chosen next use. Report time savings only if a comparable baseline and assisted attempt were actually measured; a single training exercise is not a long-term productivity study. Deliver the promised recap within one business day and log the next action.
