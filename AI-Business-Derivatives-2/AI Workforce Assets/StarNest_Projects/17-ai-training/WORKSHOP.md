# AI Workday Tune-Up — 90-minute delivery plan

Updated October 4, 2026. **Included in the proposed $200 USD pilot; price and terms await founder approval.**

## Buyer, duration, and outcome

**Buyer:** One small property-management office, up to eight attendees.  
**Duration:** **90-minute remote workshop**, with an optional separate intake of up to 15 minutes.  
**Narrow outcome:** Use three reusable templates to turn approved maintenance notes into an owner-update email, a missing-information follow-up, and an internal action summary. Human review remains required.

## Deliverables

Three prompt templates, one-page safe-use guide, three worked examples, one practice exercise, and an action recap delivered within one business day. One consolidated clarification response within seven calendar days. The templates support the same workflow; this is not company-wide AI implementation.

## Before the workshop

- Confirm date, timezone, attendees, and existing approved AI tool.
- Ask which part of maintenance communication repeats and what a good result looks like.
- Collect up to three anonymized or fictional notes. Exclude tenant names, street addresses, account balances, credentials, and confidential disputes.
- Test screen sharing and rehearse the fictional proof sample.
- Prepare static examples as an outage fallback; label them as prepared, not live outputs.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Segment | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Set the outcome and explain model limitations | Agreed task and human-review boundary |
| 10–25 | Teach task, facts, constraints, and output format | Shared business-fact and prompt structure |
| 25–50 | Practice owner update and missing-information follow-up | Two checked drafts |
| 50–65 | Practice internal action summary; assign a fresh participant case | Third template and independent practice |
| 65–80 | Build the one-page safe-use guide and audit the outputs | Team-specific review checklist |
| 80–90 | Save materials, answer questions, and choose next action | Reusable kit and optional next step |

## Teaching and acceptance checks

Every claim must trace to approved notes. Do not convert “requested,” “planned,” or “awaiting approval” into “scheduled,” “approved,” or “completed.” Separate confirmed facts, unknowns, decisions needed, and next actions. Quote amounts remain proposals unless the source says they were approved. The model must not interpret leases, invent statutory deadlines, or advise on tenant rights.

One designated participant runs a new example and explains what requires correction before sending. Track accuracy and ability to repeat the process. Measure time only if a comparable baseline and actual timed attempt exist; otherwise state “not measured.”

## Exclusions

No live tenant communications, software/inbox integration, automated sending, legal/tax advice, policy certification, confidential-data processing, unrelated workflows, or unlimited support. No recording by default. More than eight attendees or in-person delivery requires a separately agreed scope.

## One-page safe-use guide to deliver

1. Use only approved, non-confidential inputs in the selected tool.
2. Specify the audience and task before supplying notes.
3. Require a list of missing facts, not invented answers.
4. Review dates, amounts, approvals, completion status, and names.
5. Remove legal conclusions or unsupported commitments.
6. A human approves and sends every communication.
7. Save the approved prompt version and log repeated failure patterns.

## After delivery

Send templates and guide within one business day. Log delivery hours and the independent practice result. Ask for honest feedback and, separately, permission to use a testimonial. Offer the diagnostic audit only when the workshop reveals a concrete need; the buyer does not need to buy more to receive the promised materials.
