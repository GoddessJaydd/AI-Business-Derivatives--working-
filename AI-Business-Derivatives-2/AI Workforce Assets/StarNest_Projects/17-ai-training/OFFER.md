## Buyer and narrow pilot outcome

**Buyer:** A small property-management office, approximately 3–25 employees, with an owner or office manager who can approve training. Pilot attendance: up to eight people. The earlier draft also listed accounting/tax firms and small real-estate offices as future hypotheses; they are not part of this first property's example set.

**Outcome:** In a **90-minute workshop**, the team practices turning rough, non-confidential property-maintenance notes into a factual owner-update email, a follow-up draft, and an internal action summary. Participants leave with three reusable templates and review rules for that one workflow. No legal, tenancy, tax, or compliance advice is included.

**Starting geography retained from the prior draft:** Oakland/East Bay and Mariposa/Yosemite are outreach hypotheses, not researched demand or confirmed service territory. Deliver the pilot remotely to avoid travel cost and logistics; any in-person session needs separately agreed scope and travel terms.

## Deliverables

1. A short intake, up to 15 minutes, before the workshop; collect up to three fictional, blank, or anonymized examples.
2. One 90-minute remote workshop for up to eight participants from one business.
3. Three reusable prompts: owner-update email, missing-information follow-up, and action summary.
4. One-page “how we use AI here” guide: approved facts, confidential-data boundaries, human review, and escalation rules.
5. Three worked examples using one shared workflow, plus a participant practice exercise.
6. Guide, templates, and recap delivered within one business day.
7. One consolidated clarification response within seven calendar days; not unlimited ongoing support.

## Definition of done

At least one designated participant can use a template on a new anonymized note and identify unsupported dates, costs, completion claims, or commitments before sending. Each attendee receives the materials. Record any remaining practice gap honestly; attendance is not proof of mastery or business savings.

## Duration and delivery budget

Buyer workshop: 90 minutes, plus the separate optional 15-minute intake. Internal allowance: 30 minutes preparation and 30 minutes follow-up, approximately 2.75 hours including intake. Record actual hours. Retain roughly 80% standardized teaching and 20% client-specific examples; do not build a new course for each prospect.

## Exclusions

No software integration, automation, property-management-system access, inbox access or sending, confidential tenant/client data processing, website, lead generation, legal/tax/compliance advice, certification, unlimited revisions, or ongoing management. No guarantee of revenue or time saved. No recording unless explicitly agreed by attendees. Participants use their own approved AI tools; no paid upgrade is required for this scope.
